Why MaximizeMyClaim.ca

We Only Win When You Win

Our contingency fee model means zero risk for you. We invest our resources into your case and only collect when we secure your settlement.

01
Pure Contingency — Zero Upfront
No retainer fees, no hourly billing, no hidden costs. We advance all disbursements and only get paid when we win for you.
02
Ontario-Specific Expertise
Ontario's SABS, Bill 198, and provincial tort law are uniquely complex. Our lawyers know every nuance and use them in your favour.
03
Direct Lawyer Communication
Your file is handled by a called lawyer — not a paralegal. You get a direct line and regular updates throughout your entire case.
04
Proven Trial Record
Most firms settle everything. We're prepared to take your case to trial — which is exactly why insurers offer us higher settlements.
